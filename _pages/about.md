I'm a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the [University of Virginia](https://engineering.virginia.edu/departments/electrical-and-computer-engineering), advised by Professor [Jundong Li](https://jundongli.github.io/). My research centers on **LLM reasoning, memory, and distillation**, with brain disorder prediction and AI for science as secondary application areas.

My current side projects include:

- **Computing tools for Urban+Environment**: how computing tools can help with air quality and disaster management.
- **Computing tools for Brain**: how to use computing tools to understand the brain.

My current side interests:

- **Efficiency**: especially how to distill models into smaller ones.
- **Reliability**: how to make accessible models more reliable.
