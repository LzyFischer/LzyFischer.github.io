I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Virginia, advised by Prof. Jundong Li. Before that, I received my B.S. in Physics from Xi'an Jiaotong University, where I was lucky to join the LUD Lab. I have also interned at AT&T as an Applied Scientist.

My research centers on LLM continual learning, with a broad interest in graph and time series. I am open to collaborations and discussions — feel free to reach out by email or the message box! Actively looking for intern!
